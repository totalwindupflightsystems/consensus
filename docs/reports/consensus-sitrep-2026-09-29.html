Why the gap is ours, not upstream's

The reframing

The baseline compared our previous suite pin (v1.18.29) against the newer upstream (v1.18.33) and the current dev tip. The document is byte-identical across all of them:

$ sha256sum openapi-1.18.29.json openapi-HEAD.json
00502bd13e9c86f3ca9e765e99a57e06fa9f434ca16f2a714766d1444f8d37f3  openapi-1.18.29.json
00502bd13e9c86f3ca9e765e99a57e06fa9f434ca16f2a714766d1444f8d37f3  openapi-HEAD.json
$ cmp openapi-1.18.29.json openapi-HEAD.json && echo IDENTICAL
IDENTICAL

Zero paths added, zero removed, zero changed. So the ten failing upstream assertions are not protocol drift we are chasing — they are our own implementation gap, sitting still, with a fixed target. That is the best possible version of this news: a moving spec would have made the gap harder to close every week.
